# As-Is Process

## Current Loan Application Process

1. Customer contacts the bank or visits a branch.
2. Customer requests a loan application.
3. Customer completes the application form.
4. Customer gathers the required documents.
5. Customer submits the application and documents.
6. Loan Officer reviews the application.
7. Missing or incorrect information is identified.
8. Customer is contacted to provide additional information.
9. Credit Analyst assesses the application.
10. A lending decision is made.
11. Customer is informed of the decision.

## Current Problems

- Customers may need to visit a branch.
- Applications may contain missing information.
- Document verification can be time-consuming.
- Customers may have limited visibility into application status.
- Manual communication can cause delays.
- Loan Officers spend time following up on incomplete applications.
- Customers may experience a long waiting period before receiving a decision.
