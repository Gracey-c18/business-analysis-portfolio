# Stakeholder Analysis

## Project
Student Registration System

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs / Interests | Influence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Students | End users | Easy and accurate registration | High |
| Academic Advisors | Support students | Correct module and prerequisite information | Medium |
| University Administration | Manage registration | Accurate student records and reporting | High |
| IT Department | Develop and maintain the system | Clear technical requirements | High |

## Key Stakeholder Needs

### Students
- View available modules
- Check prerequisites
- Identify timetable clashes
- Submit registration
- Receive confirmation

### Academic Advisors
- View student registration information
- Check whether students meet requirements
- Assist students with registration problems

### University Administration
- Manage modules
- Manage registration rules
- Generate registration reports

### IT Department
- Receive clear and complete requirements
- Understand system processes
- Maintain system security and reliability
